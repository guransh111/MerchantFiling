Merchant Legitimacy Challenge

TrustLoop: does this merchant's money match its declared business?

Illegal betting operators open UPI merchant accounts that claim to be ordinary shops. TrustLoop finds them by comparing each merchant with similar shops in the same week, following shared phones, bank accounts and gamblers, and responding proportionately, so honest shops are left alone.

How it decides

Every step is explainable in one sentence to a merchant, an analyst or a regulator.

  1. 10 weekly signals per merchantMatch-hour share, late night, round amounts, out-of-state payers, repeat payments, amount variety, same-day pass-through, payout accounts, growth, size vs declared.
  2. Compared with peers in the same weekSame declared category and city tier. Every sweet shop triples at Diwali, so none of them looks odd. Each signal is capped, so several must agree.
  3. NetworkFronts come in families. Suspicion spreads from accounts found on public betting pages through shared phones, bank accounts and gamblers.
  4. Change points and a learning loopIt finds the week behaviour flipped, and learns from analysts' confirm/clear decisions which signals really matter.
  5. Proportionate responseNo money is held without corroboration, and no merchant is closed without a human.
