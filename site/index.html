How it decides

  1. Ten weekly signals per merchantMatch hours, late night, round amounts, out-of-state payers, same-day pass-through and five more
  2. Compared with peers in the same weekSame declared category and city tier, so festival rushes stay normal
  3. Several signals must agreeEach one is capped, so no single oddity condemns a merchant
  4. Fronts come in familiesShared phones, bank accounts and gamblers link them to known betting accounts
  5. Run as an operationCases with 72-hour deadlines and appeals, a betting-page scanner, hashed sharing between providers, draft STRs for FIU-IND

Ask before you punish

Monitor · nothing visible
Ask · a neutral question
Friction · lower limits, T+2 settlement
Review · 20% reserve, needs a network link or GST mismatch
Offboard · only after an analyst confirms

Evidence

Charts generated by the evaluation script

Honest caveat. Real transaction-level UPI data isn't public, so these results come from a simulation calibrated to NPCI's July 2025 category averages and real enforcement cases. They show the method works as designed, not real-world accuracy. The dashboard accepts a payment company's real data as a CSV or Excel upload.
